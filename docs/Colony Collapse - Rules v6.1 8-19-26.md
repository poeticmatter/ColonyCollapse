**Colony Collapse**  
**Speedrunning Democracy**  
*A game of inevitable corruption for 2-5 busy bees*  
*by Seth Jaffee and Aurore Inara… v6.1 8/19/26*

*As the leader of one of the factions of spacefaring bees from Apiary, you have found a new homeworld, and you’ve denounced your Queen in favor of a democratic form of government \-- but you’ve immediately succumbed to corruption in the form of gerrymandering in order to get elected\!*

*Win over 1-issue voters and  influence districts and you may earn enough votes to get elected governor and win the game\!*

**Components**

* 36 Hex tiles (including 6 Starting hexes)  
* 1 Hex draw bag  
* 1 Colony board (w/ Issue/Turn Order/Round tracks)  
* 30 Issue track markers (6 each in 5 player colors)  
* 5 Turn Order markers (1 each in 5 player colors)  
* 5 Player boards and screens  
* 50 Meeples (10 in each player color)  
* 30 Action tiles (6/player)  
* 36  Seed Cards

**Setup**

* Place the Colony board in the center of the table  
* Place Starting hex tiles on the board as shown. Put the rest in the hex draw bag  
* Mix the Hex tiles in the bag and deal out 6 to create a display  
* Place the Issue, Turn Order, and Round markers on the Issue, Turn Order, and Round tracks on the board  
* Give each player the following in their player color:  
  * 1 Player board (w/ screen)  
  * 1 set of 6 unique Action tiles  
  * 10 meeples in their player color  
* Randomly place the turn order markers on the turn order track, creating an initial turn order.

**You are ready to begin\!**

**Game Play** 

A game of **Apiary 2: Colony Collapse** will play out over 5 rounds. Each round will contain 4 game turns in which you will **resolve an action** in turn order. Each game turn, the actions will become stronger.

At the end of each round, there will be a polling of the districts, and you’ll score points for each district you control.

Round Setup

At the beginning of each round, fill the tile display from the bag so that there are 6 tiles.

Also, discard any seed card in the leftmost slot, then slide the remaining Seed cards to the left of the display and fill the empty spaces from the deck.

Action Programming

Using the Action tiles from your personal set, assign 4 of them to the four slots on your player board.

Resolving Actions

Once all players have assigned all of their tiles, begin resolution, starting with slot 1\.

In turn order, resolve the actions assigned to slot 1 as follows:

1. Reveal the tile you have assigned to the current slot  
2. Resolve the action according to the slot strength (1-4)

Repeat this for each slot. Once all slots have been resolved, **you may upgrade an action tile that you did not use this turn.** Flip it over to its upgraded side.

Finally, resolve a Polling (see below)

The Actions

There are 6 different actions in Colony Collapse. Each action scales in power based on its slot (1-4), which we’ll call “X.” Each action tile can be upgraded as well. The effects of each action and upgraded action are as follows:

1. **Campaign Promise**: Choose a tile from the display labeled X or less. Bump that tile’s issue track, then flip that tile over.   
     **Upgrade**: In addition, flip another tile in the display over.   
2. **Build**: Place any tile from the display into play into a district labeled X or less.  
     **Upgrade**: Then lock in the vote (lay meeple down).  
3. **Turn Order**: Advance X/2 (round up: 1/1/2/2) spaces on the turn order track.  
     **Upgrade**: Advance X spaces (1/2/3/4) instead.  
4. **Play card**: Take card from the display labeled X or less. Resolve any immediate effect on the card and place it in front of you.  
     **Upgrade**: Also advance on the Issue track matching the colored banner on that card.  
5. **Rally Support**: Advance one space on any one Issue track (max column X).   
     **Upgrade**: Go down one space on any Issue track to advance any 2 Issue tracks one space each (max column X).   
6. **Repeat Action:** Resolve an action tile in a previous slot at strength 1\.  
     **Upgrade:** Resolve an action in a previous slot as if it were in this slot.

Polling

At the end of each round, move the tile from slot 1 in the display to district 1, from slot 2 into district 2, etc. If a district is full, place the tile in the next district clockwise. Whenever a tile is in play, check its issue tracks to see who is in the lead. That player puts a meeple on the tile to make it clear who controls it. 

Then a scoring round called Polling takes place. During a Polling, do the following for each district:

1. Determine which player is ahead on each Issue track. In case of a tie, the player who got there first wins the tie. This should be reflected in the meeples on tiles (but it never hurts to double check).  
2. The player with the most meeples is leading the district and receives victory points depending on the round (ties broken by turn order, earlier is better): Award 1/1/2/2/3vp per district in round 1/2/3/4/5.

Issue Tracks

The player farthest along the Issue track for a color controls all unlocked hexes in play of that color. When multiple players are tied for the lead on a track, the player who got to that space *most recently* is considered to lead.

Starting in column 3, the first player to arrive at each space on a track may immediately lock one tile of that color \- that vote is locked in, it will not change hands if the controlling player is overtaken on the issue track.

**Game End**

After 5 rounds, the game ends.

**The player with the most victory points from controlling districts each round wins the game\!**

In the case of a tie, the tied player with the most *total* meeples in play wins. If still tied, the tied player who’s farthest along the Turn Order track wins\!
