**Colony Collapse**  
**Down with the Queen?**  
*A game of campaigning for 2-5 busy bees*  
*by Seth Jaffee and Aurore Inara… v7.1 11/02/26*

*As the leader of a faction of bees that have denounced their Queen in favor of a democratic form of government \-- you must now fight to get elected leader if the hive\!*

*Win over 1-issue voters to influence districts and you may earn enough votes to get elected leader of the hive and win the game\!*

**Components**

* 30 Hex tiles *(note: center ring hexes are printed on the board)*  
* 1 Hex tile draw bag  
* 1 Colony board (w/ Issue & Round tracks)  
* 1 Tile rondel  
* 1 Rondel pawn *(or disc you rotate to point at the tiles)*  
* 30 Issue track markers (6 each in 5 player colors)  
* 1 Start Player marker  
* 5 Player boards and screens  
* 5 player pawns (1 each in 5 player colors)  
* 75 Control discs (15 in each player color)  
* 42 Action tiles (7/player \+7 for upgrades on tracks)  
* 45 Seed Cards

**Setup**

* Place the Colony board in the center of the table  
* Mix the Hex tiles in the draw bag and deal 6 into the tile rondel, one per slot  
* Randomly assign one action tile to each issue track. When reaching space 3 on that track, you will upgrade that tile. *NOTE: One tile will not be assigned to an issue track – set it aside*  
* Place the Issue and Round markers on the Issue and Round tracks on the board  
* Shuffle the Seed cards and create a deck. Deal a seed card to each player \- seed cards indicate 2 issue tracks on them. Go up 1 space on the 1st track on your card, and down 1 space on the 2nd track. Then discard the card.

*\[note for Claude: I would like to add 6 starting seed cards to deal players instead of using cards from the deck, but those do not exist yet. Ideally they would start up and down the indicated tracks and then keep the card for an effect\]*

* Give each player the following in their player color:  
  * 1 Player board (w/ screen)  
  * 1 set of 7 unique Action tiles  
  * 15 control discs in their player color  
  * 1 Pawn (place in the center of the board)  
* Randomly assign the Start Player marker.

**You are ready to begin\!**

**Game Play** 

A game of **Colony Collapse** will play out over 5 rounds. Each round will contain 4 game turns in which you will **resolve an action** in turn order. Each game turn, the actions will become stronger.

At the end of each round, there will be a polling of the districts, and you’ll score points for each district you control.

Round Setup

At the beginning of each round, fill the tile rondel from the bag so that there are 6 tiles in it.

Action Programming

Behind your player screen, assign 4 of your Action tiles to the four slots on your player board, leaving the other three aside. All players do this simultaneously.

Resolving Actions

Once all players have assigned all of their tiles, begin resolution, starting with slot 1\.

In turn order (clockwise from the start player), resolve the actions assigned to slot 1 as follows:

1. Reveal the tile you have assigned to the current slot  
2. Resolve the action according to the slot strength (1-4)

Repeat this for each slot.

Finally, resolve a Polling (see below)

The Actions

There are 7 different actions in Colony Collapse. Each action scales in power based on its slot (1-4), which we’ll call “X.” Each action tile can be upgraded as well. The effects of each action and upgraded action are as follows:

1. **Address**: Move the rondel pawn up to X spaces clockwise on the tile rondel. Bump up 1 space on that tile’s issue track, then flip that tile over.   
     **Upgrade**: After flipping the tile, ***rally*** it (see **Rallying a Tile**, below).   
2. **Develop**: Move the rondel pawn up to X spaces clockwise on the tile rondel. ***Rally*** that tile.  
     **Upgrade**: Also ***rally*** one tile along the way (not the one you started on).  
3. **Media**: Flip any 1 tile in the rondel. It cannot be flipped by a Media action again this turn (resolving this slot).  
     **Upgrade**: Flip any number of tiles in the rondel. They cannot be flipped by a Media action again this turn (resolving this slot).  
4. **Leverage**: Draw X cards from the deck and choose one. Resolve any immediate effect on the card and place it in front of you **OR** go up one of the indicated issue tracks.  
     **Upgrade**: You may do both.  
5. **Dance**: Move your pawn X spaces on the board (from hex space to hex space, whether or not there’s a tile there). Then ***rally*** the tile you land on and all adjacent tiles (see **Rallying a Tile**, below).   
     **Upgrade**: You need only be *tied* on the issue track to gain control of rallied tiles.   
6. **Intern:** Resolve the action tile in the previous slot at strength 1, standard version.  
     **Upgrade:** Resolve the action tile in the previous slot at strength 1, upgraded version.  
7. **Invest:** Choose an issue track. Go down up to X spaces on that issue track to go up that many spaces on an adjacent track.  
     **Upgrade:** Go down up to X spaces on one track to go up that many spaces on **any** other track

Rallying a tile

When you ***rally*** a tile, that means: *if you are farther up the issue track than the player currently controlling the tile, then you gain control of the tile*. If a tile is uncontrolled, then that’s easy \- as long as your track position is **not less than 0**, you gain control of the tile (put your control disc on it). If the tile is already controlled by an opponent, then you must have a *higher* track position than that opponent to replace their control disc with your own.

Controlling a tile means that that tile will vote for you in the next polling.

Polling

At the end of each round, move the tiles from rondel to the districts, from slot 1 into district 1, from slot 2 into district 2, etc. Whenever an ***uncontrolled*** tile enters play, check its issue tracks to see who is in the lead. That player puts a control disc on the tile. If there is a tie for the lead on an issue track, then that tile comes into play uncontrolled.

Then a scoring round called Polling takes place. During a Polling, the player with the most control discs in a district receives victory points depending on the round (ties broken by control of the pre-printed center ring hex): Award 1/2/3/4/5vp per district in round 1/2/3/4/5. If no player control the center ring hex and there is a tie for control discs, then no player receives points for that district.

Note that a player pawn standing on top of a control disc temporarily assumes control of the hex. When that pawn moves, control of that hex reverts to the player with the control disc there.

Issue Tracks

The player farthest along the Issue track for a color will gain control of uncontrolled hexes of that color as they come into play at the end of the round. When multiple players are tied for the lead on a track, nobody gains control at that time. When rallying a controlled tile, the rallying player takes control of the tile if they are *higher* on that tiles issue track than the current controller (or tied with the current control in the case of an upgraded Dance action).

When reaching space 3 on each issue track, you will upgrade one of your action tiles (flip it over to the upgraded side). Which tile is upgraded on each track is determined during setup.

**Game End**

After 5 rounds, the game ends.

**The player with the most victory points from controlling districts each round wins the game\!**

In the case of a tie, the tied player with the most *total* control discs in play wins. If still tied, the tied player closer to the start player wins\!
