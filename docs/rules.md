# Colony Collapse: how to play

*The rules as the game plays them now, in plain language. The designer's rules are [Colony Collapse - Rules v7.1 11-02-26.md](Colony%20Collapse%20-%20Rules%20v7.1%2011-02-26.md); this file also covers the calls the game makes where those rules leave a question open.*

Colony Collapse is a game for 2 to 5 players. You are factions in a bee colony settling a new planet. Over five rounds, new hex tiles join the colony's six districts, and you rally support to control them. At the end of each round, whoever controls the most hexes in a district scores it. Most points at the end wins.

## What's on the table

- **The colony board:** a hub in the middle, a ring of 6 printed hexes around it (one per district), and 5 empty slots in each district, 30 in all.
- **Six issues**, each with its own colour: Clay, Sage, Periwinkle, Stone, Seafoam and Mauve, in that order around the ring.
- **Six issue tracks**, one per issue, running from −2 to +4. Everyone starts on 0 on every track.
- **30 hex tiles** in a bag. Each tile has an issue on each side; the side face up is the tile's issue.
- **The rondel:** 6 slots around a pointer, one slot per district. Tiles wait here before they join the board.
- **45 seed cards.** Each card shows two issues and an effect.
- **For each player:** 7 action tiles, a pawn that starts in the hub, and as many control discs as you need.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the tiles in the bag and put 6 in the rondel, each on a random side. The pointer starts on slot 1.
2. Six of the seven actions are matched to the six issue tracks at random, the same for everyone. The seventh is set aside.
3. Each player is dealt a seed card: go up 1 on its first issue and down 1 on its second, then discard it.
4. A start player is picked at random.

## Words you'll need

- To **rally** a hex is to compare yourself with the disc on it, on that hex's issue:
  - **No disc:** you put yours there if you're on 0 or more.
  - **Someone else's disc:** you take it over if you're higher than its owner on that issue.
  - **Your own disc:** nothing happens.
- **Control:** a hex is controlled by whoever's disc is on it. A pawn standing on a hex also gives its owner control of it while it stays there, disc or not.
- **Strength:** an action played in slot X is played at strength X.

## A round

The game lasts 5 rounds. Each round:

1. **Refill** the rondel to 6 tiles (not in round 1).
2. **Plan:** everyone at once, in secret, picks 4 of their 7 action tiles and puts them in order in slots 1 to 4.
3. **Play the slots** from 1 to 4. In each slot, going clockwise from the start player, each player reveals their tile and plays it at that slot's strength.
4. **Polling** (below).
5. **Pass the start player** one seat clockwise (not after round 5).

## The actions

| Action | What it does at strength X | Upgraded |
|---|---|---|
| **Address** | Move the rondel pointer 1 to X steps clockwise. Go up 1 on that tile's issue, then flip the tile. | Then rally the flipped tile. |
| **Develop** | Move the pointer 1 to X steps clockwise and rally that tile. | Also rally one tile it passed on the way, if you like. |
| **Media** | Flip one rondel tile, or none. | Flip any number of rondel tiles. |
| **Leverage** | Draw X cards and keep 1. Either play its effect and keep it in front of you, or go up 1 on one of its two issues. | You may do both. |
| **Dance** | Move your pawn 1 to X hexes. Then rally the hex it lands on and every hex next to it. | A tie with the disc's owner is enough to take it. |
| **Intern** | Play the tile in your previous slot again, at strength 1. It does nothing in slot 1. | The copy is played upgraded. |
| **Invest** | Go down 0 to X on one track, then up the same amount on a neighbouring track. | The second track can be any track. |

- **Media's lock:** a tile flipped by Media can't be flipped by Media again in the same slot, by anyone.
- **Pawns:** a pawn can never enter a hex with another pawn on it, and once it has left the hub it can never go back.
- **Invest's neighbours** follow the issue order; Clay and Mauve are not neighbours. You only go up as far as you actually went down.
- **Upgrades:** the first time you reach 3 on a track, your tile for that track's action flips to its upgraded side at once. The set-aside action can only be upgraded by a card.
- You can decline all or part of an action, except that the pointer and Dance must move at least 1.

Cards that stay in front of you keep working for the rest of the game. The full list of card effects is in the designer's rules.

## Polling

At the end of each round:

1. **Tiles join the board.** Each rondel tile goes to its district, on the slot for this round. If that slot is taken, it goes to the next free slot, and if the district is full, to the next district clockwise.
2. **New tiles without a disc** go to the leader of their issue: the one player highest on that track, on 0 or more. If the lead is tied, or nobody is on 0 or more, the tile stays uncontrolled.
3. **Each district scores:** whoever controls the most hexes there (its tiles and its printed hex) scores **1 point in round 1, 2 in round 2, and so on up to 5**. A tie goes to whichever tied player controls the printed hex; if none of them does, nobody scores.

## End of the game

The game ends after round 5's polling. Most points wins. If points tie, the player with the most discs on the board wins; if that ties too, the player earliest in clockwise order from the start player.
